ABOUT DYNEXAL

Learn. Build. Integrate.

Dynexal is an independent learning website focused on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and practical development. The goal is simple: make technical topics easier to understand through clear explanations, AL examples, real-world integration patterns and practical troubleshooting.

What you will find here

  • Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and AL development tutorials.
  • Tables, pages, extensions, codeunits, reports and RDLC guidance.
  • API, Shopify, e-commerce and other integration concepts.
  • AI, Copilot, Azure OpenAI, MCP and AI-agent learning resources.
  • Project ideas, implementation notes and common-error troubleshooting.

Our approach

Dynexal aims to publish useful, original and practical educational material. Examples are written to help developers understand why a solution works, not just copy a code snippet. Microsoft documentation and product behavior can change over time, so readers should verify version-specific details before using an approach in production.

Independence

Dynexal is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Shopify, Google or any other third-party company mentioned on this website.
