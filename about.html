ABOUT DYNEXAL TECHNOLOGIES

Learn. Build. Integrate.

Dynexal Technologies is an independent technology brand and learning platform focused on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, AL development, integrations, reporting and practical AI solutions.

Dynexal TechnologiesBusiness Central • AI • IntegrationsPractical technology learning, development and solution building.

What Dynexal Technologies does

  • Publishes practical Business Central and AL development tutorials.
  • Builds and documents real-world projects around APIs, integrations, reporting and automation.
  • Provides technical service capabilities around AL development, Business Central integrations, RDLC reporting and AI-focused solutions.
  • Explores developer tools, e-commerce integrations, AI agents and modern ERP architecture.

Our approach

Dynexal Technologies focuses on practical solutions rather than generic theory. The goal is to understand the business requirement, choose a maintainable technical approach, build with clear implementation steps and document what was learned.

Current focus

The current focus is Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and the technologies surrounding it: AL, APIs, OAuth 2.0, HttpClient, JSON, webhooks, RDLC, Shopify integration and AI. The platform will gradually expand into reusable tools, developer resources and technology services.
