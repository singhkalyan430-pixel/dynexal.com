Role Center Dashboard for Business Central

A focused dashboard concept that brings operational KPIs, cues, charts and navigation into a practical Business Central Role Center experience.

The challenge

Users often need to move between multiple pages to understand current workload and operational status. The objective was to create a single starting point for high-value information.

Solution approach

Business Manager Role Center → Extension → Cue cards + KPI views + Actions

The design uses page extensions and dedicated cue/dashboard pages so the Role Center can surface useful information without modifying the base application.

Key implementation areas

  • Role Center page extension
  • Cue and KPI data presentation
  • Dashboard actions and navigation
  • Operational chart concepts
  • Clean separation between data and UI

Technology stack

Business CentralALPage ExtensionRole CenterCuesKPIsCharts

Outcome

The project demonstrates how an AL extension can create a more focused user experience while keeping the solution aligned with Business Central's extensibility model.
